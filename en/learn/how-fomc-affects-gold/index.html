Gold trading Q&A

How does the FOMC meeting affect gold?

Short answer

The FOMC sets US interest rates eight times a year. Gold reacts to the surprise, not the decision itself: a more hawkish message than expected (fewer cuts, higher rates for longer) usually lifts the dollar and yields and pushes gold down; a more dovish message usually lifts gold. The statement comes at 14:00 New York time and the press conference at 14:30, and gold can swing both ways in between.

What gets released and when

  • The rate decision and statement at 14:00 New York time (02:00 GMT+7 the next day in winter, 01:00 in summer).
  • The Chair's press conference 30 minutes later.
  • Four times a year, new economic projections and the dot plot of expected rates.
  • The minutes of each meeting three weeks later.

Why the reaction can flip

Markets price the decision in advance, so gold moves on the gap between what was expected and what is said. A first spike on the statement is often reversed during the press conference when the Chair adds nuance. That is why many traders wait for the press conference to end, or at least for a 15-minute candle to close, before trusting a direction.

How to prepare

  1. Check what the market expects: the rate decision is usually priced, the guidance is not.
  2. Mark the zones above and below the current price before the release.
  3. Reduce size or stay flat into 14:00; spreads widen and slippage is common.
  4. Let the first reaction settle and see which zone holds.

Quick answers

Does a rate cut always make gold go up?

No. If a cut was fully expected and the Fed signals fewer cuts ahead, gold can fall on a cut.

How many FOMC meetings are there a year?

Eight scheduled meetings, roughly every six weeks, plus rare unscheduled ones in emergencies.
