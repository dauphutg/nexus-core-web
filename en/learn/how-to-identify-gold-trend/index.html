How to identify the gold trend (XAUUSD)

Short answer

Start on the daily chart: a series of higher highs and higher lows is an up-trend, lower highs and lower lows a down-trend, overlapping swings a range. Confirm on H4 with the last swing structure and on H1 with price versus a medium-term moving average. Trade with the higher timeframe unless a strong zone says otherwise.

Three timeframes, three questions

  • D1: what is the direction of the last few weeks?
  • H4: has the last swing broken in the same direction (a break of structure)?
  • H1: is price above or below a sloping medium-term average, and is the pullback finished?

Signs the trend is changing

  • A daily close beyond the last major swing high or low.
  • H4 failing to make a new extreme and breaking the other way.
  • The H1 trend average flattening and price crossing it repeatedly.

Range days

When the timeframes disagree, gold is often ranging. The NEXUS CORE plan then says "two-way" with a lean, and trades the edges of the range instead of the middle.

Quick answers

Which single indicator shows the trend best?

Price structure: highs and lows, is the most direct; moving averages help visualize it.

How long does a gold trend last?

It varies from days to many months; that is why the daily chart leads.
