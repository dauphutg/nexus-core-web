How to trade gold in a sideways market

Short answer

In a sideways market, gold rotates between a clear upper and lower boundary. Sell near the top zone and buy near the bottom zone, take profit before the opposite edge, avoid the middle, and keep the invalidation just outside the range, because when a range finally breaks, it can move fast.

Recognizing a range

  • Highs and lows stop progressing on H1 and H4.
  • The trend average flattens and price crosses it often.
  • Daily ranges shrink below the 10-day average.
  • Calm before big news is common.

How to trade it

  • Mark the range edges as zones, not lines.
  • Look for rejections (long wicks, failed breakouts) at the edges.
  • Aim for the middle or just before the opposite edge.
  • Stand aside when price is in the middle.

When the range ends

A close beyond the edge, especially with news or session momentum, ends the range strategy. Flip to breakout rules or wait. NEXUS CORE marks such days as two-way, with a lean when one side looks stronger.
