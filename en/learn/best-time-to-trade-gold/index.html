Gold trading Q&A

What is the best time to trade gold? Asia, London and New York sessions

Short answer

Gold is usually most active when London and New York are both open — roughly 13:00 to 17:00 UTC in winter (one hour earlier in summer) — and around US data releases at 8:30 New York time. The Asia session is typically calmer and often builds the range that London and New York later break.

The three sessions

  • Asia (Tokyo, Hong Kong, Singapore, Sydney): lower volume, ranges form, reactions to overnight news.
  • London: volume jumps at the London open (08:00 London time); the first real direction of the day often starts here.
  • New York: US data at 8:30 ET, the COMEX gold futures open and the Fed. The London–New York overlap is usually the most volatile part of the day.

Timing tips

Many intraday traders prepare during Asia, look for the first move at the London open, and reassess before New York. Large moves often come in the first 30–60 minutes after major US numbers. The last hour before the daily close (17:00 New York) tends to be thin.

On US bank holidays liquidity drops — see the page on trading gold on holidays.

Quick answers

Can I trade gold on weekends?

Spot gold and CFDs close from Friday 17:00 New York time until Sunday evening; prices can gap at the reopening.

Is the Asia session bad for gold?

Not bad — just slower. It suits range trading; breakout traders usually wait for London.
