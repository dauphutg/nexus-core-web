Gold trading Q&A

What is XAUUSD?

Short answer

XAUUSD is the price of one troy ounce of gold quoted in US dollars. "XAU" is the currency-style code for gold and "USD" is the dollar, so a quote of 4050.00 means one ounce costs 4,050 dollars. Traders use XAUUSD to speculate on gold rising or falling without owning physical metal.

How the symbol works

Gold is traded like a currency pair. XAU is the base, USD is the quote. When XAUUSD rises, gold is getting more expensive in dollar terms — either because demand for gold is up, the dollar is weaker, or both.

Most retail platforms offer XAUUSD as a spot CFD that trades almost 24 hours a day from Monday to Friday, with a short daily pause around 17:00 New York time. Some brokers list it as GOLD or add a suffix such as XAUUSD.m.

How big is a move?

Gold is usually quoted to two decimals. On many brokers one standard lot equals 100 ounces, so a 1-dollar move in XAUUSD is worth about 100 dollars per standard lot, 10 dollars per mini lot (0.1) and 1 dollar per micro lot (0.01). Always check your own broker's contract size.

Gold often moves 20 to 60 dollars in a single day, and much more around major US data. That is why position size matters more on gold than on most currency pairs.

What moves XAUUSD

  • The US dollar: a stronger dollar usually weighs on gold.
  • Interest rates and real yields: higher yields raise the cost of holding a metal that pays no interest.
  • US data such as CPI inflation and the jobs report (NFP), and Federal Reserve decisions.
  • Risk mood and geopolitics: gold is a classic safe-haven asset.
  • Central bank buying and physical demand.

Quick answers

Is XAUUSD the same as gold futures?

No. XAUUSD is usually a spot price (or a CFD on it). Gold futures such as COMEX GC trade on an exchange with fixed expiry dates. Prices are close but not identical.

Why is it called XAU?

XAU comes from the ISO 4217 currency-code system, where precious metals get an X code; AU is the chemical symbol for gold.
