How to use the daily open line when trading gold

Short answer

The daily open is the price at which the current daily candle started (17:00 New York time on most platforms). Above it, buyers are in control of the day so far; below it, sellers are. Price often comes back to it during the day, which makes it a useful intraday reference for bias.

Three ways traders use it

  • Bias filter: prefer buys above the open and sells below it, unless a strong zone says otherwise.
  • Reference: in quiet sessions price often drifts back toward the open before the next move.
  • Reaction level: the first retest of the open often produces a bounce or rejection.

Previous opens matter too

Yesterday's and this week's opens that price has not revisited yet are widely watched reference levels. Treat them like any other level: one input among several, never a signal on its own.

Limits

On strong trend days price can leave the open behind and never return. On news days the open can be crossed many times. Use it as one input among the zones, not as a stand-alone signal.

Quick answers

Which time zone defines the daily open?

Most forex and CFD brokers start the gold daily candle at 17:00 New York time; check your platform's server time.

Is the weekly open useful too?

Yes — the Sunday open of the week is a widely watched reference for weekly bias.
