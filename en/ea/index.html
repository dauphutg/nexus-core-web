Questions

Do you need my broker password?+

No. The EA runs inside your own MT5 terminal. We never ask for your broker login or password.

Which broker can I use?+

Any broker that offers XAUUSD on MetaTrader 5. A hedging account and a VPS, so MT5 stays online 24/5, are recommended.

Can I test it first?+

Yes. Demo accounts are free, so you can watch the EA trade before using real money.

Can I use it on several accounts or move my licence?+

Your licence covers 1 live account and 3 demo accounts. Extra live accounts are a monthly add-on, and you can move a slot to another account up to 2 times a year.

What happens if my connection drops?+

Every position has a stop-loss at your broker, so it stays protected while you are offline. If only our licence server is briefly unreachable, the EA keeps running and managing your open trades for a while.

Why is there a monthly fee?+

The EA opens trades from fresh market data our server publishes every day, plus updates and support. That service has a monthly fee, or a lower yearly fee. If you stop paying, the EA keeps managing open trades but stops opening new ones; pay again and it continues, with no need to buy the licence again.

Can I get a refund?+

If the EA does not work because of a fault on our side and we cannot fix it, you can ask for a refund within 14 days. We do not refund trading losses. Your consumer rights are not affected.
