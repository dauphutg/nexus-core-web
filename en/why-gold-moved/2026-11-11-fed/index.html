Why gold moved · Wed, Nov 11

Gold fell 1.18% as hawkish Fed remarks and a firmer dollar erased early gains

Day change-1.18% (-47.7)
Open → close4035.39 → 3987.7

What moved it

  • Fed Governor Bowman said inflation is broadening and there is more work to do on rates; Dallas Fed President Logan called for two more hikes. CME FedWatch priced an 87.1% chance of a 25 bp hike at the 8–9 December meeting.
  • The US dollar index rose 0.65% to 102.89. Gold reached 4067.1 early in the day, then slid to 3982.68; the biggest hourly drop, 22.71 dollars, came in the hour starting 9:00 ET as New York opened.
  • It extended the slide that began after Tuesday’s October CPI rose 0.4% m/m, above expectations; spot gold closed the day back under 4,000 at 3987.7.
