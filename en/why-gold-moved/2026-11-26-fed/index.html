What moved it

  • Gold opened at 3583.51 and dipped to 3551.34 early in Asia as Reuters reported hot US inflation cementing Fed hike bets; Dow Jones also cited rate-hike bets.
  • Prices rebounded to 3607.24 by 02:30 UTC; Reuters later said gold steadied after an over 1-year low, but Dow Jones noted US inflation data kept fueling rate-hike bets.
