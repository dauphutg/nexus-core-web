How plans are graded

  1. Every tradeable zone in a plan is graded on its own on 15-minute XAUUSD candles (OANDA feed): the main zone plus every other zone with a published invalidation level. Watch-only zones and zones that are only in play after a break are not scored.
  2. Each plan is graded on its own session: from publication until the next update replaces it, or the daily close (17:00 New York) for the last update. A zone counts only if price trades into it during that session.
  3. Once reached, the zone is followed until the end of that session and settled there: Target 1 first → “Target 1 reached”; a 15-minute close beyond the zone’s invalidation level first → “Invalidated” (−1R). Neither → “Closed at session end”, scored at the session’s last close.
  4. R = distance from the zone edge to Target 1 (or the closing price) divided by the distance from the zone edge to the invalidation level. When both happen in the same candle, it counts against the plan.
  5. Carry-over is mechanical: a trade still open at the end of its session (no Target 1, no invalidation) is always handed to the next update of the day — whether or not that update repeats the zone — and followed until the end of its session with the same entry, levels and R, counted once, there. The last update of the day settles everything at the daily close. A plan’s R is the sum of its zones; wins and losses are counted alike, and the totals show R from Target 1 / invalidation separately from R closed at session end.
  6. Prices are OANDA 15-minute candles; spread, slippage and fees are not included.
  7. Stand-aside plans are not scored.
  8. MFE (maximum favourable excursion) is shown separately: the furthest price travelled in the zone’s direction from its edge, from the touch until invalidation or the daily close, measured from the zone’s conservative edge. It shows how good the read was — it is never added to R. MFE is shown in dollars and pips (1 pip = 0.10 $); the monthly figure is the average in dollars over all zones reached. MFE is also expressed in standard R (1R = 10 $), a fixed unit for comparison — not the trade’s own R.
  9. Near-zone reaction (added 14 Nov 2026): a zone missed by 2 $ or less inside the plan’s window is labelled with the closest distance and the move that followed. It is a label only — the zone still counts as not reached and nothing is added to R.
  10. Rules v3 final, 14 Nov 2026 (graded and settled per session, open trades always carried to the next update). Every earlier plan was re-graded once under these rules. The rules are now frozen: any later change applies to new plans only.
